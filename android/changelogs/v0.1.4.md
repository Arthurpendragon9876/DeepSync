## Yugen v0.1.4

**Cloudstream plugins**, a new **Override global defaults** page where Manga, Novel and Streaming can each keep their own settings, **customisable reader bars** for the manga and novel readers, and a player with **swipe gestures** and a **Skip Opening** button. It installs over 0.1.3-patch.2 and keeps your library.

### Added
- **Cloudstream (.cs3) plugins.** Settings > Extensions > **Cloudstream plugins** installs Cloudstream plugins next to your Aniyomi extensions. Each provider of a plugin shows up as an anime source, so it works in Explore, Home, favourites, history and the player like any other.
  - **Add a repository** from a repository URL, a `cloudstreamrepo://` link or a short code, then browse **Available plugins** (with search) and install from there. A repository that is down is marked, and one that cannot be read tells you why.
  - **Install from URL** (a direct link to a .cs3) or **Install from file** (pick one from your device).
  - **Installed plugins** list the providers each one adds and why one failed to load. Plugins can be updated (**Update to vN**), removed, or reloaded with **Reload plugins**.
  - YouTube plugins work: the YouTube extractor Cloudstream uses is bundled.
  - A plugin that crashes no longer takes the app with it, and a plugin settings screen that tries to restart the app reloads the plugin instead.
- **Override global defaults** (Settings). Each area has its own switch: off, every mode shares one value; on, **Manga, Novel and Streaming each keep their own**. The mode picked at the top of the page is the one being edited, so you don't have to enter a mode first.
  - **Homepage**: banner, carousels, tracker, source catalogue and filters. "Custom homepage for each mode" now lives here; the Home settings page only says which Home you are editing.
  - **Explore**: *Open on Discover*, *Discover as a list*, *Discover tracker* (or "As Home") and *Open Discover titles with* the tracker's page or straight to your source.
  - **Services**: related titles, reading / watching statistics, reading-time estimation, what happens when a title is not tracked yet (Ask, Closest match, Exact match), *Sync progress to trackers* and *Keep trackers in step*.
  - **Favourites**: whether favourite categories stay in every mode.
  - **Updates and Feed**: show updates from every mode, or only the mode you are in, separately for the Updates page and the Feed page.
  - **History**: show entries from all modes, or only the current one.
  - **Suggestions**: take the titles from your **sources** or from **AniList**.
- **Suggestions from AniList.** With AniList as the source, Suggestions uses AniList's recommendations for what you read or watched and looks each one up in your installed sources, so every suggestion is a real, openable title. If nothing is found (offline, nothing matched) it falls back to your sources. TV & Movies always uses sources, since AniList has no shows or movies.
- **Customise the reader's top and bottom bars.** Settings > Reader > **Customise reader bottom bar and top bar** (also reachable from the reader's quick settings) gives every button one of four places: **top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right**. Switch buttons on or off, drag to reorder, and use **Move to** to send one to another bar. Chapters and Settings can move too, and the page slider stays between the two bottom sides. The previews on top are the real bars. A bar you customised in an earlier version is kept.
  - New bar buttons: **Reader settings**, **Open in browser** (built-in WebView), **Open in external browser** and **Bookmark**. The bookmark button is now a bar button and is gone from the old menu.
  - Buttons on the top bar use the same translucent, rounded segment look as the toolbar's actions.
- **Customise the novel reader's bars.** Settings > Novel reader > Controls > **Customise novel reader bars** does the same for the novel reader: Chapters, Previous, Next, the **chapter progress slider**, web buttons (only for online novels), Highlight, Search, Rotate, Auto scroll, Listen and Settings. Settings always stays on, because it opens the panel you change this from.
- **Seasons in the episode list.** When an entry has two or more seasons, a **season picker** appears above the episodes, with a count for each season. It follows you into the next season while you watch, and the "S2 E5:" prefix is dropped from episode names once seasons are shown.
- **Sub / Dub also filters the episode list.** Episodes that cannot be played in the chosen audio are hidden, with a hint saying how many and a button to **show all**. What an episode offers is learned when you play it, so the list gets more accurate as you watch. Previous / Next in the player skip hidden episodes too.
- **Swipe gestures in the player.** In fullscreen, swipe up or down on the **right half for volume** and on the **left half for brightness**. A level bar shows the value while you swipe. Brightness only affects the player and resets when you leave it. Switch it off with **Swipe gestures** in Settings > Player.
- **Skip Opening button.** When the episode has known timestamps (AniSkip), a **Skip Opening / Skip Ending / Skip Recap** button appears while that part plays and jumps to the end of it. At the end of an episode it becomes **Next Episode**. It hides itself after ten seconds, and does nothing for anime that has no timestamps. Switch it off with **Skip opening prompt** in Settings > Player.
- **MangaBaka lists.** With MangaBaka as the metadata source, the list you pick now takes effect: **Popular**, **Trending** (biggest climb in the last 7 days) or **Recent**. Title pages from MangaBaka show the **publication status, chapter and volume counts and the authors and artists**.

### Changed
- **TV & Movies tagging is smarter.** A Cloudstream plugin is tagged by the content types its providers declare (Movie, TV series, Anime...). An Aniyomi extension is scored from its name, package, sources and the hosts of its sources, so a weak word such as "stream" can no longer decide on its own. Extensions that were tagged automatically are judged again with the new rules; **a tag you set yourself is never changed back**. The tag button also works on Cloudstream plugins.
- **Explore follows the mode and the half of Streaming.** Changing mode, or flipping between Anime and TV & Movies, resets the filters and reloads Explore instead of leaving the old mode's titles until you refresh. Coming back from Settings re-applies *Open on Discover* and the tracker you chose.
- **TV & Movies Discover always uses Simkl**, so the page is no longer blank when Home uses another tracker or the source catalogue.
- The "Reader controls in bottom bar" setting is now called **Customise reader bottom bar and top bar**.

### Fixed
- **MangaBaka's popular list showed "unknown title" rows.** MangaBaka's popularity is a rank (1 is the best), so the list was sorted the wrong way round and started with the least popular series. Stub entries without a real title or cover are also skipped.
- **Discover could end up empty on Simkl.** A genre filter that Simkl cannot apply on its side could leave the page with nothing; the unfiltered feed is shown instead.
- **Preferences changed in Settings now apply as soon as Explore is shown again**, including list view and the Discover tracker, without reloading.

### Under the hood
- The Cloudstream library is bundled as a jar with its Android-only classes removed; the build checks its SHA-256 and fails if it does not match. New R8 keep rules cover the Cloudstream and NewPipeExtractor APIs that plugins link against, so release builds can load plugins.
- Unit tests for the episode season / audio parsing and for the TV & Movies classifier.
- New **Android release test build** workflow: builds the real signed release APK and attaches it to the run without creating a tag or release, so it can be tested on a phone first. The patch workflow gained a **reset_to** option, so a newer revision of a patch applies cleanly after an earlier one.
