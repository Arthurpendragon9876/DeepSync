## Yugen v0.1.3-patch.2

A patch on top of 0.1.3-patch.1: Yugen gets a **Streaming** mode with a TV & Movies half, **Simkl** as a tracker for it, and a redesigned manga reader bar with a new quick settings panel. It installs over 0.1.3-patch.1 and keeps your library.

### Added
- **Streaming, with Anime and TV & Movies.** The Anime slot in the mode fan is now **Streaming**. While your thumb rests on it, it splits into an **Anime** half and a **TV & Movies** half, and a pill shows which one you are on (for example "Streaming · TV & Movies"). In the tap-opened fan, tap Streaming to split it and tap again to pick one.
- **Streaming default** (Settings > Home layout): Off keeps the split. Pick Anime or TV & Movies and the fan shows that one directly, with its own icon and name, and never splits.
- **Tag an extension as TV & Movies.** Installed anime extensions have a tag button that flips them between anime and TV & Movies. A new extension is tagged TV & Movies automatically when its name says movie, cinema or stream without any anime word. Your own choice is never changed back.
- **Foldable extension sections.** Installed anime extensions are grouped into **Anime** and **TV & Movies**, each with a Hide / Show toggle. The TV & Movies section only appears once an extension is tagged.
- **Simkl.** Sign in to Simkl to track shows, movies and anime, and to search and browse TV & Movies metadata. Simkl is the tracker for TV & Movies: it is not offered on anime or manga pages, where AniList, MyAnimeList and the others still apply.
  - **Continue on Simkl** carousel on Home, and Simkl as a Home metadata source.
  - The tracker library has two tabs, **Anime** and **TV & Movies**.
  - Sign-in returns through `com.kotatsu.deepsync://oauth/simkl`.
- **Quick settings in the manga reader.** The Settings button in the bottom bar opens a panel just above the bar. While it is open the button turns into a close button, and the back button closes the panel.
- **Bookmark button in the reader's top bar.** It toggles the bookmark for the current page and shows whether it is already added. It is hidden in landscape and in the novel reader.

### Changed
- **The manga reader's progress bar is now a capsule**, drawn like the novel reader's: a rounded rail that fills as you read, with a thin marker at your position. It swells while you touch it. The page preview while dragging works with it as before.
- **The bottom bar's Settings button is always visible** and uses a new tune icon. Chapters stays first and Settings stays last, as in the novel bar. The old three-dot menu in the top bar is gone.
- **Home, Explore, History, Feed, Search and Stats follow the half of Streaming you are on.** Anime titles only show on Anime, TV & Movies titles only on TV & Movies. Suggestions and genre affinity are kept apart the same way, so anime never feeds TV & Movies picks.
- **TV & Movies has its own Explore.** It uses its own genre list (Action to Western) and formats (Series, Movie). The anime and manga tags such as BL, Yuri, Harem and Isekai are hidden there. When no extension is installed, you see "No TV & Movies extension installed".
- **Home on TV & Movies** has no AniList or MyAnimeList data, no tracker sync row and no anime categories. It uses Simkl metadata, unless you chose the source catalogue. The new-episodes row is called **New releases**.
- **Better matching for movies and shows.** A source title is checked against the first release year, movie or series, and country of origin, so a 2026 film is no longer linked to its 1997 namesake, and a movie never matches a series.
- **Release tags are ignored when matching.** Things like Season 1-2, 720p, HEVC, Dual Audio and language tags in a source's title no longer stop it matching the real name.
- **Smarter source search.** A title is also searched without punctuation and cut at its first colon or dash ("Dexter: Resurrection" also searches "Dexter Resurrection" and "Dexter"), so sources with poor search still find it. The extra searches only find candidates and never count as a match on their own.
- **Novel reader TTS option renamed to "TTS floating icon".** It now only controls the round Listen button. The **TTS** mode always stays in the quick settings, and turning the icon off no longer stops something that is playing.
