## Yugen v0.1.3-patch.1

A patch on top of 0.1.3: in-app updates are back, and the novel reader gets a new bottom bar, reorganised quick settings and chapter header options. It installs over 0.1.3 and keeps your library.

### Added
- **In-app updates.** Yugen checks this repository's releases and offers new versions on the home screen and in the About settings, including patches like this one. A patch is a normal signed release, so it installs over the version you have.
- **Chapter controls in the bottom bar.** The bar is now Chapters | previous chapter | progress capsule | next chapter | Settings. The **|<** and **>|** buttons jump to the start of the previous or next chapter. Dragging or tapping the dotted **capsule** seeks inside the chapter you are reading.
- **Rotate screen** in the quick settings: one tile that locks or unlocks the orientation and shows which of the two is on.
- **Name only** (on by default): the chapter header shows just the chapter's name, so "Chapter 12: Name" becomes "Name".
- **Remove redundancy** (Off / Number / Name / Both): removes the chapter number, the name or both from the first line of the text when it only repeats the header. Scroll mode only.
- Both chapter header options are in Settings > Novel reader, in a new **Advanced chapter header** group, and in the reader's quick settings.

### Changed
- **Quick settings are reorganised.** A mode selector and tiles for Auto scroll, Text & font, Rotate screen and Color & theme sit at the top, with **Advanced reader options**, **Presets** and **Reset** below. The **Reading**, **More** and **Extra** pages replace the old tabs.
- **Text-to-speech is now opt-in.** The Listen button is a round floating button above the bar and only appears after you turn on **Options > Text-to-speech**. If you used Listen before, switch it on once.
- **Long chapter titles wrap** over up to three lines in the header instead of being cut off.
- **Softer taps in the reader.** Bottom bar buttons show a halo behind the icon while pressed, and the top bar's web button now shows press feedback too.

### Fixed
- **Changing the line height or font size no longer moves your place** in the chapter.
- **Jumping to a search result or a saved position lands in the right spot** when the header is taller than usual because of a wrapped title.
